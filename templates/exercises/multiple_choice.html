How confidence-based scoring works

For each option, you must choose exactly one confidence level:

  • right = "I’m sure this statement is true."
  • not sure = "I’m 50-50."
  • wrong = "I’m sure this statement is false."

The computer then scores each choice like this:

Your mark If the option is really... Points
rightcorrect+2
rightincorrect−2
not sureeither0
wrongincorrect+1
Wrongcorrect−1

Why this system?

  • It rewards you (+2) for being confidently right.
  • It penalises you (−2) for confident mistakes—so guessing is risky.
  • You can play it safe with "Not sure" (0 points), but you won’t earn much.

Bottom line: You score highest by stating your true level of certainty.
