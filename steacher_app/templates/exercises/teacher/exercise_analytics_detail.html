About these analytics

This is a tool to help you understand the uncertainty of the AI tutor's responses. It is based on the perplexity of the AI tutor's responses. The perplexity is a measure of how uncertain the AI tutor is about choosing the next token. The higher the perplexity, the more uncertain the AI tutor is about the next token.

It is computed based on the LLM's log probabilities of the tokens in the response. It's not perfect, but it's cheap and a good starting point.
