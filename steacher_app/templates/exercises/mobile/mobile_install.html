Install Steacher

Add Steacher to your home screen for a faster, app-like experience. No app store required!

Or follow the manual instructions below

iOS (iPhone/iPad)

1. Open in Safari
Make sure you're viewing this page in Safari

2. Tap the Share button
Tap the Share icon at the bottom

3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen"

4. Confirm installation
Tap "Add" in the top right corner

5. Launch from home screen
Find the Steacher icon on your home screen and tap it

Android (Chrome)

1. Open Chrome menu
Tap the three-dot menu in the top right

2. Tap "Install app"
Look for "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen" in the menu

3. Confirm installation
Tap "Install" in the popup dialog

4. Launch from home screen
Find the Steacher icon on your home screen and tap it

Need Help?

Can't find "Add to Home Screen"?
Make sure you're using Safari on iOS or Chrome on Android

Already installed but can't find it?
Check your home screen or app drawer for the Steacher icon
